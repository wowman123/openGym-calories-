# Food database sources

Nutrient values per 100 g, from:

- **USDA FoodData Central (FNDDS, Foundation Foods, SR Legacy)** (13358 foods). Public domain (CC0 1.0). https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/
- **McCance and Widdowson's Composition of Foods Integrated Dataset 2021 (Public Health England)** (2852 foods). Open Government Licence v3.0. https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/composition-of-foods-integrated-dataset-cofid
- **Standard Tables of Food Composition in Japan 2015 (Seventh Revised Edition), MEXT** (2191 foods). Government of Japan Standard Terms of Use (attribution). https://www.mext.go.jp/en/policy/science_technology/policy/title01/detail01/1374030.htm

Built by scripts/food-db/build.mjs. Values are as published; no changes beyond rounding.
